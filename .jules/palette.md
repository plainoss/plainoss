## 2025-05-18 - Inline Confirmation for Destructive Actions in Drawers

**Learning:** Destructive bulk actions (such as clearing measurement history) placed inside drawers or sidebars can be easily triggered by accident on touch or small screens. Replacing immediate execution with a 2-step inline confirmation toggle ("Confirm Clear" / "Cancel") protects user data without introducing disruptive full-screen modals.
**Action:** Always provide inline confirmation states for destructive list/history clearing actions in secondary panels.
